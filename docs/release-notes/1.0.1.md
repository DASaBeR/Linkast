# Linkast 1.0.1

A fix for cameras that looked smooth in Linkast but ran at 1 or 2 frames per second on stream. Also clearer messages when a stream can't start because the game window is minimized.

## Fixed

- **Smooth cameras on stream when your PC is busy.** If streaming used most of your computer's power (for example a demanding game at 60 fps), the camera on stream could drop to 1 or 2 frames per second while the preview in Linkast stayed smooth. Cameras and web overlays now keep their full frame rate even when the stream can't quite keep up.
- **"Could not start streaming to the server in time" when the server was never the problem.** If the game or window you capture sends no picture, usually because it is minimized, Linkast now says so and keeps trying until you switch back to it. The error opens the Capture settings, not the Network settings.

## New

- **A notice when the captured window is minimized.** Windows sends no picture of a minimized window, so the stream can't start, or shows a frozen picture, until you switch back to the game. Games in exclusive fullscreen minimize whenever you switch to Linkast, so you may see this often with them.
- **More useful logs.** While you are live, Linkast writes the frame rate, repeated and dropped frames, encoder speed and upload rate to its log once a minute, plus a summary when a stream ends. If something looks wrong on stream, the log from `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Linkast\logs` now shows when it happened and why.

## Known limitations

- An encoder speed below 1.0× in the log means your PC can't encode the stream in real time. Viewers may see the stream buffer. Try 30 fps, a lower resolution, or fewer overlays.
- The limitations of [1.0.0](1.0.0.md) still apply.
