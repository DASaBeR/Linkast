# Linkast 0.2.0-beta.1

The first public beta. Linkast is a lightweight, open-source live-streaming app for Windows. It runs entirely on your PC: no account, no cloud, no telemetry.

## What you can do

- **Stream** your screen, a window or a game, with system audio and your microphone, to Twitch, YouTube or any RTMP/RTMPS server. Hardware encoding (NVIDIA, AMD, Intel) is used when available.
- **Choose the network for your stream,** for example gaming on Ethernet while streaming over a phone or 5G modem. Linkast verifies the network actually used and never switches silently.
- **Webcam Studio:**
  - shapes, borders, picture controls and presets;
  - background blur, removal or replacement with local AI;
  - auto-framing;
  - animated and voice-reactive borders;
  - up to four cameras;
  - landscape and vertical (9:16) layouts.
- **Honest health:** you are only shown *Live* when frames are encoded and reaching the server. Errors say what went wrong and how to fix it.

## New in this beta

- **Linkast Camera** (Windows 11): use your Linkast look (blur, auto-framing, picture settings) in Zoom, Teams, Discord, Meet or OBS. Press **Set up Linkast Camera…** on the Webcam tab; Windows asks for administrator permission once.
- **New look.** The new Linkast logo, and dark mode by default. Light, or matching Windows, is one click away under **Appearance** at the bottom of the window.
- **Installer.** Per-user, with no admin rights, and updates from GitHub Releases. Linkast checks once at start-up and when you press **Check for updates**. A new version is shown next to the button and is installed only when you press **Update now**.
- **The stream key is no longer passed to FFmpeg.** It used to be visible in Task Manager's command-line column; now the key is added by Linkast's own network relay.
- **Smaller download.** FFmpeg is now the pinned, verified 9.0.1 "essentials" build. Its source is published with this release; the few libraries whose exact source the build doesn't identify are listed in its `SOURCES.txt`.

## Known limitations

- **Not code-signed yet,** so Windows SmartScreen may warn: More info › Run anyway.
- **Tested hardware so far:** only an NVIDIA RTX 3070 with a phone used as a webcam. AMD and Intel GPUs, other webcams and real platforms are in the [field test plan](../field-test-plan.md); reports are very welcome.
- **Exclusive-fullscreen games** may capture as black; use borderless windowed mode.
