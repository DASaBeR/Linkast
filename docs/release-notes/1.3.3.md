# Linkast 1.3.3

Much better microphone noise removal, a stream that recovers properly after a network drop, and sound that stays with the picture when the upload struggles.

## New

- **New noise removal.** The microphone is now cleaned by a small neural network trained on speech (RNNoise), running on your PC. It removes fans, hum, hiss, keyboards and street sound, also while you talk, not only between words. Choose **Remove noise**, or **Remove noise and breaths** to also lower what is left between your words. It costs some CPU: about 3 % of one core while live (on a 6-core CPU that is well under 1 % of the whole processor). Your voice comes out 10 ms later, too little to notice. It can be changed while live.

## Fixed

- **Noise removal did nothing unless you switched it while live.** A choice made before going live never reached the stream. It now applies from the first second.
- **After a network drop, the stream kept dropping again.** Each automatic reconnect was cut again after 11 seconds as "stalled", even when it was working, and the server then refused the next quick reconnect. Reconnects now stay up.
- **Changes made while live were undone by a reconnect.** Overlays you moved or hid, a game you switched to, and volume changes jumped back to how they were when you went live, and a game you had closed came back. A reconnect now keeps everything as you left it.
- **Sound far behind the picture on a slow upload.** When the upload could not keep up, Linkast paused the encoder, and sound and picture came apart (up to 40 seconds in one log). Now, when more than 8 seconds are waiting to be sent, the oldest seconds are skipped: viewers see a short jump, and sound and picture stay together. The warning says how much was skipped and which bitrate your connection can carry.
- **Sound slowly drifting ahead of the picture** after a moment where the PC was too busy to deliver a frame. The sound now drops the same time as the picture.
- **A second stream with another resolution** (for example after switching to a vertical scene) could send pictures of the old size. Each stream now uses its own size.
- **Overlays changed between streams** showed as they were the first time. They are now made fresh for each stream.

## Logs

The 30-second lines now show how far the sound is from the picture (behind or ahead), how much picture was lost and why, how often the sound's output had to wait, sound a channel lost because it could not be mixed in time, noise removal's real CPU share, and how much of the stream was skipped for a slow upload. The summary counts every connection, not only the last. A layer moved while live is logged, with a warning when that layer is not part of the running stream.
