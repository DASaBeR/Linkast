# Linkast 1.0.3

"Only the game's sound" works now, changing sources while Linkast connects no longer locks out your stream key, your game should keep more of its frame rate while you are live, and Linkast's log now says much more about your PC and your stream when you report a problem.

## Fixed

- **Going live with "Only the game's sound" failed** with "At most four audio sources are supported" when you had two or more alert boxes. Up to 16 sounds can go into a stream now.
- **The server refused the stream key after you changed something while Linkast was connecting.** Turning on "Only the game's sound", or choosing another game, during those first seconds restarted the connection halfway, and Restream and others then held on to the stream key and refused the next connection for a while ("The stream key may be wrong or already in use"). Linkast now waits until you are live, then switches cleanly.
- **A clearer message when you go live again quickly.** If the server still holds your previous connection, Linkast says so and waits a little longer before trying again, instead of suggesting the key is wrong.

## Changed

- **Less load on your game while live.** A game window larger than the stream (for example 1440p or 4K into a 1080p stream) is now scaled down on the graphics card before Linkast copies it, so it copies 44 to 75 % less data each frame. Linkast's encoder also no longer runs at a higher priority than your game.

## New

- **Much more useful logs** (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Linkast\logs`, or Show log › Open logs). When Linkast starts it writes your Windows version, CPU, memory, power (battery or plugged in), each graphics card and its driver, each monitor (resolution, refresh rate, scaling, HDR) and the Windows game settings. When you go live it writes what is captured: the game window's size, whether it is fullscreen, borderless or windowed, and its monitor. Every minute while live it writes the frame rate your game really delivered, and how busy the CPU and graphics card are. Everything in the Activity list is in the log too.

## Known limitations

- The limitations of [1.0.2](1.0.2.md), [1.0.1](1.0.1.md) and [1.0.0](1.0.0.md) still apply.
