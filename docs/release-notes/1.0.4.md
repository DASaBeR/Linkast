# Linkast 1.0.4

Your game and your camera are smooth on stream now, sound stays with the picture, and Linkast tells you when your upload can't carry the bitrate you chose.

## Fixed

- **The game looked like 15 fps to viewers** although it ran at 60 or more. While Linkast waited for the streaming server (it takes a few seconds to answer when you go live, and an upload can have slow moments), it held back the game capture, and Windows threw away the game's frames meanwhile. Linkast now keeps the finished stream in memory and sends it on separately, so the capture never waits for the network: a game at 67 fps reached the stream at 16 fps through Restream before, and at 56 to 58 now.
- **The camera was choppy on stream** (about 8 pictures a second) while it looked smooth in Linkast. Its pictures are now taken on their own and kept ready for the stream, so all 30 a second arrive.
- **Sound fell behind the picture**, more and more during a stream, and Linkast could then use several GB of memory and stop. Sound and picture now stay together (within a few hundredths of a second) and the delay no longer grows.

## New

- **A warning when your upload can't keep up.** If your connection can't send the bitrate you chose, the Activity list says so ("The upload is not keeping up … Lower the bitrate"). Viewers would otherwise fall further and further behind. Lower the bitrate, or let **Find the best settings** test your upload and suggest one.

## Tips

- **An echo of your own voice** (a word repeating more and more quietly) means the stream is playing on your PC, for example in a Kick, Twitch or Restream tab, while Linkast captures your desktop sound. Mute that tab while you stream, or watch on your phone.
