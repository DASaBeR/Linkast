# Linkast 1.0.2

Put your sources in the order you want: drag the game, cameras and overlays to stack them, scene by scene, and drag scenes to reorder them. Also a mixer that shows what viewers hear, a Test sound that follows its fader, and a switch to stream only your game's sound.

## New

- **Stack your sources in any order.** Under **Sources in this scene**, the game, your cameras and your overlays are now one list in the order they are drawn: the first is at the back, the last in front. Drag a row up or down to change it, or right-click it for **Bring forward** and **Send back**. The preview follows at once.
  - **Each scene has its own order,** so a camera can be over the game in one scene and behind an overlay in another.
  - **While live:** switching scenes stays instant when their orders agree. Changing the order while live, or switching to a scene that stacks the same sources the other way round, pauses the stream for about a second while it restacks.
- **Drag scenes to reorder them** in the Scenes list. Move up and Move down are still in the ⋯ menu. A scene now switches when you release the click, so starting a drag no longer puts it on air.
- **Only the game's sound.** A switch at the top of the Audio tab (when you capture a window): viewers hear the game, your microphone and your alerts, and nothing else. A video in your browser, Discord or a notification sound stays off the stream. It works while live; turning it on or off may pause the stream for a second.

## Fixed

- **The mixer's meters show what goes into the stream.** They used to show each sound before its fader, so muting Desktop audio or turning the microphone down left the meter moving as before, and the mixer looked broken. Now a muted sound's meter drops to nothing and a lower fader shows a lower level. Your viewers hear a change a few seconds later than you make it, because Kick and other platforms delay the stream.
- **An alert's Test sound follows its fader.** The chime used to play at the same volume whatever the fader said, so it sounded as if the fader did nothing. It now plays at the fader's volume, so you hear it as your viewers will, and a muted alert stays silent.

## Changed

- **A scene's background is always at the back.** A background picture or video used to be drawn over the game; now the game, cameras and overlays are always in front of it.
- **Every volume change and mute you make while live is written to the log,** with whether the stream applied it.

## Known limitations

- The limitations of [1.0.1](1.0.1.md) and [1.0.0](1.0.0.md) still apply.
