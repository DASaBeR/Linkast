# Linkast 0.3.0-beta.1

A redesigned Linkast. Every feature from 0.2.0-beta.1 is still here; it is now easier to find, easier to read, and built around the Linkast colours. Linkast still runs entirely on your PC: no account, no cloud, no telemetry.

## New look

- **Navigation rail.** Stream, Webcam, Settings and About are on the left. The tabs and the footer are gone.
- **Output preview.** The Stream page shows what viewers will see: your screen with your cameras on top. **Edit layout** takes you straight to the layout editor.
- **One clear action.** **Go live** is the brand-gradient button. While you are live it becomes a red **End stream** button, and the LIVE badge and timer stay in the header on every page.
- **Stream health.** Before you go live, a short summary of what will be sent. While live, six tiles: frame rate, upload, encoder speed, dropped frames, reconnects and the network actually used.
- **Network for your stream** is now a list of your connections with icons, so choosing the stream's network is one click. **Test connection** sits right under it.
- **Audio meters** for system audio and the microphone. The microphone is only measured while you have it switched on.
- **Sliders show their values** (for example 115%, 1.25×, −32 dB), and many have a reset button.
- **Problems** appear as cards with what happened, how to fix it and, for network problems, a **Test connection** button. Technical details are folded away.
- **Settings and About** are full pages: appearance, updates, diagnostics, privacy, the GPL licence and the open-source notices.
- **Dark, light or Match Windows,** with text contrast checked against WCAG AA in both themes.

## Webcam Studio

- Three sections: **Camera & effects**, **Layout** and **Linkast Camera**, instead of one long page.
- Each camera's look, background, framing and effects are tabs next to its preview.
- The **layout editor** has a layer list with show/hide per layout, the selected camera's position and size in output pixels, and one-click placement (corners, centre, full screen).
- A camera without a preset now shows **Custom look** instead of an empty box.

## Known limitations

- **Not code-signed yet,** so Windows SmartScreen may warn: More info › Run anyway.
- **Tested hardware so far:** only an NVIDIA RTX 3070 with a phone used as a webcam. See the [field test plan](../field-test-plan.md); reports are very welcome.
- **Exclusive-fullscreen games** may capture as black; use borderless windowed mode.
