# Linkast 1.3.4

Apps on their own fader, a stronger microphone boost, sound that stays with the picture, and a game picture that comes back after Windows ends the capture.

## New

- **Apps on their own fader.** In the Audio tab, under **Apps on their own fader**, pick a program such as Spotify, a browser or Discord (programs playing sound now are listed first). Its sound, and that of its child processes, gets its own fader, mute and level meter. It works while live, and the program is found again when it restarts. Windows can leave only Linkast itself out of Desktop audio, so an app on its own fader is also in Desktop audio: the tab warns about it, and muting Desktop audio gives each app one fader.
- **Microphone boost, up to +30 dB.** It raises a quiet microphone before noise removal, like a preamp, and works while live. If your microphone was too loud with Windows' boost and noise removal together, turn Windows' boost down and use this one: it clips less and leaves the noise removal less to fight.
- **A limiter on the mix.** Loud sounds together (a game, your microphone turned up, an alert) used to pass full scale and were cut off, which sounds harsh. The whole mix is now turned down smoothly to stay just under full scale.

## Fixed

- **Sound out of step with the picture now and then.** After a capture hiccup, or with a sound card or USB microphone whose clock runs a little fast, up to 200 ms of sound stayed waiting and was heard late for the rest of the stream. Each sound now keeps about 20 ms waiting and plays a little faster (0.5 %, not audible) to get back there. A slow clock is stretched the same way, which removes the short gaps it caused.
- **A black picture after a game switched display mode.** When a game changed between fullscreen and windowed, or changed resolution, Windows could end the capture while the window stayed open, and the stream stayed black even after switching scenes. The capture now starts again by itself within a second.
- **A game picture that cut out showed the layers under it, or its last picture.** A game or screen that is lost, minimized or restarting is now black in its place, and the empty parts of the canvas are black instead of dark blue.
- **A game that restarted stayed silent on its own fader.** Games are now followed by program, so a restarted game is heard again.

## Logs

The 30-second lines now say when the mix was too loud and by how much it was turned down, how much sound was played a little faster or stretched to stay with the picture, the microphone boost, and when an app's sound was found again or its program closed. The going-live line lists the boost and the apps with their own fader. A capture that starts again is logged.
