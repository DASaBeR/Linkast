# Linkast 1.3.6

Manage all recording options in their own **Recording** tab, and edit your vertical recording live side-by-side with the main stream preview.

## New

- **Dedicated Recording tab.** All recording settings have moved out of **Quality** into a new **Recording** tab organized into **When I go live**, **Vertical recording**, and **Files**, with a live summary banner showing exactly what will stream and save when you go live and an updated tooltip on the **Record** button.
- **Editable vertical preview on the main page.** When vertical recording is enabled, Linkast shows an interactive 9:16 vertical preview next to your main stream preview (both scaled to match height), with top-left output chips (`Stream · W×H` and `Vertical recording · W×H`) and a red **REC** indicator on whichever outputs are actually recording.
- **Live vertical layout editing.** Drag, resize, and trim cameras, overlays, and the captured game or display independently in the vertical preview both before and during a recording (`SetCanvasLayoutAsync`), without disturbing your landscape stream or switching the active scene.
- **Safe zones, quick layouts, and visibility controls.** Toggle **Show safe zones** to preview areas covered by TikTok/Reels/Shorts buttons and captions (guides only, never recorded), apply quick arrangements (**Game top · camera bottom**, **Camera top · game bottom**, **Full game · camera corner**, **Full FOV · centered (16:9)**), switch game scaling between **Fit** and **Fill** or **Reset game pan & zoom** from **Layout ▾**, and show or hide the vertical preview from the eye button or the **Recording** tab while recording continues.
- **Pan & zoom inside the vertical game box (`Pan game`).** Toggle **Pan game** on the vertical preview header to drag the captured game across your full FOV (`Fill` layouts automatically initialize from the currently visible slice) and scroll the mouse wheel to zoom in or out without moving the box.
- **Draggable vertical frame on the main preview (`Frame on stream`).** Toggle **Frame on stream** to overlay a draggable, corner-resizable dashed **Vertical frame** box over the full-FOV **Stream** preview that stays synced two-way in real time with the vertical scene (`… vertical.mkv`) and is never recorded to your landscape stream.
- **Empty, vertical, and narrow-window states.** Shows clear prompts when no vertical scene exists (**Add a vertical scene**) or when the active scene is already vertical, displays a movable **Game not running** placeholder if the captured game is closed, and automatically shrinks to a compact strip or expandable toggle when the window is narrow.

## Fixed

- **GPU Fit/Fill parity and startup capture inheritance.** `MediaOutputScheduler` now honors `BaseFit.Fit` and `BaseFit.Fill` on D3D11 using the capture texture dimensions so recorded vertical `.mkv` files match the preview, and default scenes cleanly inherit the configured Stream display or window at startup.

## Updated

- **Media pipeline and settings.** `D3D11StreamEngine` supports live vertical `CanvasLayout` updates via `SetCanvasLayoutAsync("vertical", ...)` preserved across reconnects; `StreamSettings` remembers **Show vertical preview**, **Show safe zones**, and **Frame on stream** (`ShowVerticalCropFrame`); error fix links for recording and vertical issues now open the **Recording** tab directly.
