# Linkast 1.3.5

Record at a different quality from the stream, and record a vertical canvas at the same time as your landscape stream.

## New

- **Separate recording quality.** In Settings › Recording, choose **Recording quality** to keep **Same as stream (no extra work)** or record at **1080p**, **720p**, **480p** or **1440p** with its own variable-bitrate (VBR) bitrate. Stream at 720p while recording 1080p (or 480p), without changing your stream quality.
- **Simultaneous vertical recording (Shorts, Reels, TikTok).** Turn on **Also record vertical** in Settings › Recording, pick a vertical scene, size (**1080 × 1920** or **720 × 1280**) and bitrate, and Linkast writes a `… vertical.mkv` file alongside your landscape stream or recording. Switching your main landscape scenes while live (for example to *Be right back*) leaves the vertical recording showing its vertical scene without restarting the stream.
- **Isolated recording failures.** Extra recordings run in separate encoder processes: if a disk fills up or your graphics card refuses another encode session, the recording stops with a warning while your live stream continues.

## Fixed

- **480p picture shearing.** The 480p preset now uses `852 × 480` (divisible by 4, required by the NV12 conversion shader) and existing `854 × 480` settings map automatically.
- **Apps on their own fader.** Ended processes that Windows still lists while another handle is open are now ignored when following an app's sound.

## Updated

- **Media pipeline and diagnostics.** Extra recording outputs are isolated per process (`RecordingOutput`), `D3D11StreamEngine` logs active outputs and per-output frame counts, and `ErrorClassifier` reports full-disk and GPU encoder session-limit errors clearly.

## Notes and limits

- **Shared audio mix:** in this version, the vertical recording shares the active stream's audio mix and scene mutes.
- **Live edits:** edits made to the vertical scene while already live apply to the next recording; switching between landscape scenes stays instant.
- **GPU work:** each separate recording uses an extra encoder session and GPU readback (a warning appears when using the CPU `x264` encoder). Network reconnects close the current MKV files and start new ones on the next attempt.
