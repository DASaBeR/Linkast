# Linkast 0.5.0-beta.4

Scenes and a real audio mixer. Set up your game, cameras, alerts and pictures once, then switch between scenes such as Starting soon, Gaming, Just chatting, Be right back and Ending, instantly and without restarting the stream. Every sound gets its own fader, like in OBS.

If you are coming from 0.5.0-beta.1, also see the [0.5.0-beta.3 notes](0.5.0-beta.3.md): dragging overlays and the game picture in the preview, the OBS import improvements and more.

## New

- **Scenes.** A strip of scenes sits above the Output preview. Click one to switch; while live the switch is instant, because everything every scene uses is already in the stream. **+** adds a ready-made scene (Gaming, Just chatting, Starting soon, Be right back, Ending, or an empty one); right-click a scene to duplicate, move or delete it. A scene decides:
  - whether the game or screen shows, and where (move, resize and Alt-trim it in the preview);
  - where each camera goes, and which overlays show;
  - its background: the Linkast card with your own words, a picture, or a video that loops (the preview plays it too);
  - which sounds are silent while it is on.
- **Start and end like a show.** On the new Scenes tab, Go live can start on your Starting soon scene, and End stream can show your Ending scene for a few seconds and then end by itself. Press End stream again to end at once.
- **Scene hotkeys** that work in games: Ctrl+Alt+1 to 9 switch to that scene, Ctrl+Alt+Left and Right go through them. Change them in Settings.
- **Audio mixer**, on a tab of its own (Audio). A fader (0 to 200 %, with dB), a mute and a level meter for desktop audio, the microphone, every alert or web overlay, and every scene video. Everything changes the stream instantly while live.
  - **Alerts have their own fader.** Each web overlay runs in its own small browser, so you can turn one alert down without the others. With your default output device, desktop audio leaves alerts out, so they are not heard twice.
  - **Game sound on its own fader** (Audio tab): when you capture a window, its program gets a fader of its own. Its sound is also part of desktop audio, so turn one of the two down.
- **The Be right back screen is now a scene,** with your picture, words and "mute PC sound" choice carried over. The Be right back button and hotkey switch to it and back to where you were.

## Changed

- Your layouts became scenes: "Landscape (16:9)" is now **Gaming** and the vertical layout **Gaming (vertical)**; Starting soon, Just chatting, Be right back and Ending are added. An overlay that was switched off is hidden in every scene. Nothing else changes in your setup.
- An overlay's **Show** switch is now **In this scene**: each scene remembers which overlays it shows. Position and size stay the same in every scene.
- The **Activity** card's buttons sit side by side, with a clear Show log toggle.
- The Stream page tab **Source & audio** is now two tabs, **Source** and **Audio**.

## Known limitations

- **Not code-signed yet,** so Windows SmartScreen may warn: More info › Run anyway.
- **New this release and not yet tested in long real streams:** live scene switching with real cameras and alerts, the game's own sound, each alert's own sound, and a scene video's sound. Please report anything odd with the log from `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Linkast\logs`.
- A new or changed scene background shows on stream the next time you go live. Switching between landscape and vertical scenes still restarts the stream for a few seconds.
- Each web overlay uses about 100 MB of memory for its own browser.
- **Recording starts with the stream.** To record a stream, tick **Also record when I go live** before going live.
- **Discord shows your own camera mirrored** in its preview, including Linkast Camera. Others in the call see it the right way round.
