# Linkast 0.4.0-beta.1

The streamer essentials: alerts and overlays, a Be right back screen, recording, hotkeys, importing from OBS and a quality check. Linkast stays small and local: no scenes, no plugins, no account, no cloud.

## New

- **Stream page tabs.** The output preview stays on top. Below it, the setup is split into **Source & audio**, **Destination**, **Network**, **Quality** and **Overlays**, so there is no long scroll.
- **Platform presets.** Pick Twitch, YouTube, Kick or Facebook and the server is filled in. **Where do I find my key?** opens the platform's page with your key. Custom still takes any RTMP or RTMPS server.
- **Microphone mute and noise removal.** Mute works instantly while live (the stream does not restart), and the header shows MIC MUTED. Noise removal (FFmpeg's built-in filter, no download) cuts fan and keyboard hiss.
- **Alerts and web overlays.** Paste the link from Reymit, Streamlabs, StreamElements or any alert service, the same link you would use as an OBS Browser Source. Alerts play with their sound through System audio. Links count as secrets: they are saved encrypted and kept out of logs. They need the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, which Windows 10 and 11 normally have.
- **Pictures on stream.** A logo or frame at any of nine positions, any size. Show, hide and resize live.
- **Be right back screen.** One click covers the game, cameras and overlays with a branded card or your own picture. It can also mute game sound while your microphone stays on. Use it as "Starting soon" too.
- **Record to a file.** Use **Record** to save without going live, or tick **Also record when I go live** to keep a copy of every stream. The same encoding is used for both, so recording costs almost nothing extra. Files are MKV, which survives a crash; **Make an MP4 copy** turns them into MP4 when they end, without re-encoding. Recording alone shows REC, never LIVE.
- **Global hotkeys** that work while a game has the focus: Ctrl+Alt+L go live or end, M mute, C cameras, B Be right back, R record. Change them in Settings › Hotkeys. Linkast warns when another app already uses a key.
- **Import from OBS.** Settings › Import from OBS reads your OBS profile and current scene. It brings over the server and key, resolution, frame rate, bitrate and encoder, the captured screen or game window, cameras with their places, alert links and pictures, audio, and the recordings folder. You tick what to bring over; anything Linkast can't take (text, media files, other source types) is listed with the reason. OBS itself is never changed.
- **Find the best settings** on the Quality tab. Linkast tests each encoder on your PC, then suggests resolution, frame rate and bitrate. On Twitch it measures your upload with Twitch's test mode (nothing goes live); for other platforms you type in your upload speed. One click applies the suggestion.
- **New artwork:** the refreshed Linkast icon and logo.

## Fixed

- **Test pattern and "Webcam only" streams** now go out at real-time speed. Before, FFmpeg could generate these pictures faster than real time and send a platform several times the chosen bitrate. Screen and window capture were not affected.

## Known limitations

- **Not code-signed yet,** so Windows SmartScreen may warn: More info › Run anyway.
- **Tested hardware so far:** only an NVIDIA RTX 3070 with a phone used as a webcam. See the [field test plan](../field-test-plan.md); reports are very welcome.
- **Exclusive-fullscreen games** may capture as black; use borderless windowed mode.
- **Recording starts with the stream.** To record a stream, tick **Also record when I go live** before going live. Starting a recording in the middle of a stream would briefly interrupt it, so Linkast doesn't offer that.
- **The Twitch upload test needs your stream key** on the Destination tab.
