# Linkast 1.0.0

The first stable release. Linkast has a new workspace: your scenes and sources on the left, a large preview in the middle, and going live on the right. Everything from the 0.5 betas is still here, just easier to find.

If you are coming from an earlier version, also see the [0.5.0-beta.6](0.5.0-beta.6.md) and [0.5.0-beta.4](0.5.0-beta.4.md) notes: scenes, the audio mixer, per-scene games and overlays.

## New

- **A three-panel Stream page.** Drag the gaps between the panels to resize them; the preview gets the rest. The panels keep the width you give them when you maximize or restore the window, and Linkast opens maximized.
  - **Left: scenes and sources.** Click a scene to switch to it (instant while live); its ⋯ button or a right-click has Duplicate, Move up, Move down, Scene settings and Delete. Under it, **Sources in this scene** lists the game or screen, every camera and every overlay. The eye on each one shows or hides it in this scene only. Click a source to select it in the preview, double-click it for its settings, right-click it for more. **Add** adds a camera, an alert box or a picture.
  - **Middle: the preview, much larger,** with the settings below it. Drag the bar between them to give the preview more room. The tabs are grouped: this scene (**Capture**, **Audio**, **Overlays**, **Scene**) and the stream (**Destination**, **Network**, **Quality**).
  - **Right: going live.** Go live or End stream, and Record, Mute and Be right back next to it. Then **Before you go live**, a short checklist with a link to fix each missing step, a summary of where and how you stream with a **Change** link for each, and the live numbers once you are streaming.
- **Errors say where to fix them.** The error card has a button that opens the right settings, for example **Open Destination settings** when the server address is missing. An empty server or stream key field says what to put there.
- **Settings that can't change while live say so** at the top of the tab, instead of only being greyed out.
- **Questions in Linkast's own look.** Restarting the stream, capturing a whole display, deleting a scene and removing a camera or an overlay ask first, in a dialog that says what will happen. The safe button is selected for anything that deletes.
- **Webcam Studio is balanced:** a large 16:9 camera preview with Camera on, Device and Resolution right under it, and Look, Background, Framing and Effects beside it. It uses the whole window width.
- **On/off switches** for settings that turn something on, such as System audio, Microphone, Linkast Camera and Keep my face in frame.

## Changed

- **Audio tab:** the mixer is at the top, the sound devices below it. The mouse wheel scrolls the page over the mixer too; Shift + wheel scrolls the faders sideways.
- **Scene tab:** only the scene's name, background, and how streams start and end. Showing or hiding sources and silencing sounds per scene moved to the eyes under Sources and the mixer's middle button.
- **Header:** smaller, with the name of the page you are on. Settings and About fill the window.
- **Record** is greyed out while live instead of disappearing. Copy report and Open logs are under **Show log**.

## Known limitations

- **Not code-signed yet,** so Windows SmartScreen may warn: More info › Run anyway.
- **The new workspace has not been tested in long real streams yet,** and not at Windows display scaling of 125 % or more. Please report anything odd with the log from `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Linkast\logs`.
- The preview has no zoom; it always fits the panel.
- Panel widths are remembered while Linkast runs, not after a restart.
- The limitations of 0.5.0-beta.6 still apply: at most 4 games, windows or displays in one stream, and about 100 MB of memory per web overlay.
